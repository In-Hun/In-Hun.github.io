About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the School of Cybersecurity at Korea University and a member of the Cryptographic Algorithm Lab. My research focuses on the security of cryptographic algorithms, with particular interests in side-channel analysis and fault injection attacks. I am especially interested in blind side-channel analysis, implementation security, and the security evaluation of post-quantum cryptography implementation.
